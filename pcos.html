Side project · Women’s health

PCOS: asking what the data from a diverse population can show.

Women’s health is under-researched, and polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a common endocrine and metabolic condition. This is a side project alongside my neuroscience work. To my knowledge it is the first analysis to examine co-occurring conditions, age at diagnosis and genetic markers together in a diverse population.

Diverse populationNot limited to a single ancestry.
