Final step of creatine synthesis

GAMT

GAMT catalyzes the last step of creatine biosynthesis, transferring a methyl group from S-adenosylmethionine to GAA to form creatine. GAMT deficiency is a cerebral creatine deficiency syndrome. My iPSC-derived neuron work on GAMT is described on the Home page.
